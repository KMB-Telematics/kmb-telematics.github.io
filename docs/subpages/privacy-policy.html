This policy explains what personal information KMB Telematics Inc. ("KMB", "we", "us") collects through this website, www.kmbradar.com, why we collect it, and the choices you have. KMB is a Delaware corporation headquartered in Washington, DC, and is responsible for this information.

What we collect

You can browse this website without giving us your name or contact details. We collect the following when you submit one of our forms:

FormInformation you provideWhy we use it
ContactName, email address, subject, messageTo respond to your inquiry
Brochure downloadName, email address and, if you choose, company, job title and phone numberTo provide the brochure and keep a record of who requested it
Updates signupEmail addressTo keep you on our list for KMB updates

With each submission we also record your IP address, your browser's user agent (its type and version) and the date and time. We use these to protect our forms from spam and abuse and to keep them working properly.

We do not currently send regular updates. If we begin, each message will include a way to unsubscribe. You can ask us to remove your address at any time.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in a message. The login form on our Resources page is not active: anything entered there is not sent to us or stored.

Who receives your information

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Stored submissions are used only by KMB. We rely on these service providers to run the website and our forms:

We also send form submissions to Mattermost, an internal team chat that KMB operates, to alert our staff. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of our business.

How long we keep it

  • Form submissions are kept for up to 60 months and then deleted. This includes the related team chat notifications.
  • Technical logs that we control are kept for up to 30 days.

We may keep information longer if the law requires it or if it is needed to resolve a dispute.

Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

We process your information on these legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests: responding to business inquiries, understanding interest in our products, measuring use of the website, and protecting it from abuse.
  • Steps before a contract: where you ask about buying or using our products.
  • Consent: signing up for updates. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

KMB and its service providers are based in or operate in the United States, so your information will be transferred there. Where required, we rely on standard contractual clauses for these transfers, as set out in the data processing agreements of AWS and Airtable. You can request a copy of these safeguards by emailing info@kmb.ac.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EEA, UK, Switzerland, Canada and certain U.S. states, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to obtain a copy of it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to withdraw consent.

To make a request, email info@kmb.ac. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within the time the law requires. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

This website uses HTTPS, and we limit access to stored submissions to KMB personnel. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. To report a security issue, choose "Report a vulnerability" on our Contact page.

Children

This website is intended for business and professional audiences. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to make it noticeable.
